# Outreach — community post drafts

Drafts for the human to review, adapt, and post. Ground rules (from CLAUDE.md):

- **Retype every post in your own words — never paste a draft.** (Rule added
  after a moderator-lens review, S5.) The drafts are briefs. Mods click
  profiles and compare posts across subs; near-identical phrasing across
  venues reads as a template campaign, and polished AI-style prose next to an
  AI-disclosure line invites "so what did the human do?" Your imperfect
  sentences are the feature, not the bug.
- **Only claim what is true of you.** The drafts are deliberately neutral
  about your life — no flock details, no origin stories. Add your real ones
  wherever you can (they're the best material), and never post a biographical
  claim you can't back up in a live reply. If you don't keep chickens, tell
  Claude — the honest framing changes and that's fine.
- **Transparent maker-posts only.** Never hide that you made the tool. The drafts
  include a light "built this as a free experiment, AI helped with the heavy
  lifting" line — honest, disarming, and it heads off "is this a content farm?"
  suspicion. Trim it if a community reacts badly, but never claim the tool is
  something it isn't.
- **Read each community's rules before posting.** Self-promotion rules change.
  If a sub requires mod approval or has a weekly self-promo thread, use that
  route. If in doubt, message the mods first — a mod who says yes is also a mod
  who won't delete the post.
- **Stagger the posts.** One community per day or two, not all at once. Watch the
  first one and respond to every comment before posting the next.
- **Feedback is the product.** Requests and complaints in the comments are next
  session's roadmap — paste anything interesting into the ledger's parking lot.
