**Thread title:**
Made three free calculators for chicken planning — sharing in case they're useful

**Body:**

After one too many rounds of feed-bag arithmetic, I turned the three
calculations every chicken keeper repeats into proper little tools, free for
anyone:

https://jbourgogne.github.io/homestead-toolshed/

1. **Flock size** — how many hens for your household's actual egg consumption,
   sized so winter doesn't leave you buying store eggs in January
2. **Coop size** — square footage for coop and run (bantam/standard/heavy,
   cold-climate adjustment), roost length, nest boxes — or check how many birds
   an existing coop honestly holds
3. **Egg cost** — what your eggs really cost per dozen once feed, bedding, and
   the coop are counted, next to the store carton

No ads, no signups, nothing tracked — they run entirely in your browser, and
every formula is shown on the page with its sources. Being upfront: I keep the
chickens; an AI assistant helps a lot with the building. Corrections from
people with more seasons behind them than me are the most useful thing I can
get — if a number looks off, tell me and I'll fix it.
