**Thread title:**
Made three free calculators for chicken planning — sharing in case they're useful

**Body:**

After one too many rounds of feed-bag arithmetic, I turned the three
calculations every chicken keeper repeats into small free tools, for anyone
who wants them:

https://jbourgogne.github.io/homestead-toolshed/

One works out how many hens cover your household's actual egg consumption,
sized so winter doesn't send you back to the store in January. One does coop
and run square footage (bantam, standard, or heavy birds, with a cold-climate
adjustment), plus roost length and nest boxes — or in reverse, how many birds
an existing coop honestly holds. And one figures what your eggs really cost
per dozen once feed, bedding, and the coop are counted, next to the store
carton.

No ads, no signups, nothing tracked — they run entirely in your browser, and
every formula is shown on the page. An AI assistant helps a lot with the
building, which seems worth saying plainly. Corrections from people with more
seasons behind them than me are the most useful thing I can get — if a number
looks off, tell me and I'll fix it.
