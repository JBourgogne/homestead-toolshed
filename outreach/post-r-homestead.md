**Title:**
I made a free calculator that tells you what your backyard eggs *actually* cost (feed, bedding, coop and all)

**Body:**

Every winter the same thread shows up: someone does the feed-bag math,
announces their eggs cost $9 a dozen, and by spring nobody cares. I built the
calculator version of that argument:

https://jbourgogne.github.io/homestead-toolshed/egg-cost/

You put in your flock size, lay rate, feed price, bedding/extras, and
(optionally) the coop cost spread over its lifespan, and it gives you a cost
per dozen — plus how that compares to the store carton over a year. There's a
breakdown of where the money goes and a "how this is calculated" section that
shows all the math.

It's free — no ads, no signup, no tracking; everything runs in your browser.
An AI assistant does a lot of the heavy lifting on the build side; the project
is a small experiment in making free tools for this community. If it's useful,
I'd love to know what number it gave you. If it's wrong, I'd love to know that
more.

There are two more chicken calculators on the site (coop & run sizing, and
flock sizing) if those are more your question.
