**Title:**
I made a free calculator that tells you what your backyard eggs *actually* cost (feed, bedding, coop and all)

**Body:**

Every winter someone in my life does the math on a feed bag and announces the
eggs are costing us $9 a dozen, and every spring we decide we don't care. So I
finally built the calculator version of that argument:

https://jbourgogne.github.io/homestead-toolshed/egg-cost/

You put in your flock size, lay rate, feed price, bedding/extras, and (optionally)
what the coop cost spread over its lifespan, and it gives you an honest cost per
dozen — plus how that compares to the store carton over a year. There's a
breakdown of where the money goes and a "how this is calculated" section that
shows all the math, because a calculator that hides its math is just an opinion
with buttons.

It's completely free — no ads, no signup, no tracking, everything runs in your
browser. It's the first tool in a little experiment of mine: building genuinely
useful single-purpose tools for this community (with an AI assistant doing a lot
of the heavy lifting, honestly). If it's useful, I'd love to know what number it
gave you. If it's wrong, I'd love to know that more.

There's also a coop & run size calculator on the site now (the 4-sq-ft debate,
made consistent). What should the next one be? Current shortlist: raised-bed
soil volume, rainwater harvest potential, feed cost projector.
