How this is calculated

No magic — just the arithmetic you'd do on the feed bag, done consistently:

feed $/mo = hens × lb/day × 30.4 ÷ bag lb × bag $
eggs/mo = hens × eggs/wk × 4.35
cost per dozen = total $/mo ÷ (eggs/mo ÷ 12)

If you include your coop and gear, that one-time cost is divided evenly across the years you expect it to last, and that monthly slice is added in. That's the difference between the two numbers people argue about: the operating cost of a dozen, and the all-in cost.
