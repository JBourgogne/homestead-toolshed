How this is calculated

Per-bird space, using the roomy community standard rather than bare minimums (crowding is the root of pecking, feather-pulling, and most "why are my hens mean" threads):

Bird sizeCoopRunRoost
Bantam2 sq ft5 sq ft8 in
Standard4 sq ft10 sq ft10 in
Heavy5 sq ft12 sq ft12 in

Cold winters add 1 sq ft per bird to the coop — birds that won't walk on snow spend whole days indoors, and winter is when crowding turns into bullying. Free-ranging doesn't change the coop number (they all sleep inside regardless), but the run figure becomes a fallback for bad-weather days rather than a daily requirement.

Nest boxes: one per 4 hens (12×12×12 in), always at least one, and they'll still all queue for the same favorite. For the record: poultry extension space-allowance guidance sets the minimum at 3–4 sq ft per bird indoors plus 10 sq ft outdoors — this tool plans at the roomier end of that, because minimums are where pecking problems start. When in doubt, build bigger; nobody has ever complained their coop was too easy to clean.

This tool covers space planning only — for ventilation, predator proofing, and flock health, your local cooperative extension service has excellent free guides.
