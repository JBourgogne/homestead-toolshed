About this place

The Toolshed is a small collection of calculators for questions that come up over and over in homesteading communities. Every tool explains exactly how it calculates its answer, cites where its default numbers come from, and runs entirely on your device.

Nothing here is safety guidance — for food preservation, animal health, and anything where getting it wrong could hurt, talk to your local cooperative extension service.

Built with care, given away free. Suggestions and corrections welcome.
